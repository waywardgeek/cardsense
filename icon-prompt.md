# Icon Design Prompt for cardsense

Create a clean, modern app icon for "CardSense" - an accessibility tool that helps low-vision Magic: The Gathering Arena players by identifying cards on screen and reading them aloud via text-to-speech.

## Design Requirements:

**Core Concept**: A Magic card silhouette combined with accessibility symbols (eye/vision + sound/speaker)

**Visual Elements**:
- A simplified playing card outline (rounded corners, vertical rectangle)
- An eye symbol or vision-related icon overlaid or integrated into the card
- Sound waves or speaker icon to indicate text-to-speech functionality
- Clean, minimal design that works at small sizes (16x16 to 512x512 pixels)

**Style**:
- Flat design or subtle gradient
- Modern and professional but friendly
- High contrast for visibility
- Icon should be immediately recognizable as card-related + accessibility

**Color Palette** (choose one):
Option 1: Blue gradient (accessibility/tech) with white card outline
Option 2: Purple/violet (Magic: The Gathering brand association) with white accents
Option 3: High contrast - dark background with bright green/cyan accents for visibility

**Layout Suggestions**:
- Central card silhouette with eye symbol in the middle and sound waves emanating from the side
- Or: Card outline with half showing an eye, half showing speaker waves
- Or: Circular badge design with card + eye + sound elements arranged symmetrically

**Technical Specs**:
- Square canvas (1024x1024 recommended for macOS .icns generation)
- Transparent or solid background (transparent preferred for .app icons)
- Simple enough to be recognizable at 32x32 pixels
- Should look good in both light and dark UI themes

**Avoid**:
- Copyrighted Magic: The Gathering card art or logos
- Too much detail (won't scale down well)
- Text (except possibly "CS" monogram if very subtle)

Generate a single, polished icon design suitable for a macOS application that helps low-vision gamers experience Magic: The Gathering Arena through intelligent card recognition and text-to-speech.
